# User Guide - FYP_LAM Application

## 📱 Getting Started

Welcome to FYP_LAM (Legal Agreement Management), your comprehensive solution for creating, managing, and digitally signing legal agreements. This guide will walk you through all the features and functionality of the application.

## 🚀 First Time Setup

### 1. Application Launch
When you first open the application, you'll see:
- **Splash Screen**: Displays the NDA logo while the app loads
- **Automatic Navigation**: The app checks if you're already logged in
  - If logged in → Goes directly to Dashboard
  - If new user → Shows Welcome Screen

### 2. Account Creation
**Step 1: Welcome Screen**
- Tap "Sign Up" to create a new account
- Tap "Login" if you already have an account

**Step 2: Registration Form**
Fill in the required information:
- **Full Name**: Your legal name
- **Email Address**: Valid email for account verification
- **Phone Number**: Contact number with country code
- **Date of Birth**: Your birth date
- **ID Number**: Government-issued ID number
- **Password**: Secure password (minimum requirements apply)
- **Confirm Password**: Re-enter your password

**Step 3: Verification**
- You may receive an OTP (One-Time Password) via email
- Enter the verification code to activate your account

## 🏠 Dashboard Overview

After successful login, you'll access the main dashboard (Page21) which displays:

### Dashboard Features
- **Agreement Counter**: Total number of your agreements
- **Status Filters**: View agreements by status:
  - 📝 **Draft**: Incomplete agreements you can continue editing
  - ⏳ **Pending**: Agreements waiting for signatures or approval
  - ✅ **Complete**: Finalized and signed agreements

### Agreement List
Each agreement shows:
- **Title**: Agreement name/description
- **Creation Date**: When the agreement was created
- **Status Badge**: Visual indicator of current status
- **Quick Actions**: Tap to view, edit, or manage

## 📄 Creating New Agreements

### Method 1: From Templates
1. **Navigate** to agreement creation section
2. **Select Template** from available options:
   - Employment Agreement
   - Lease Agreement
   - Sales Agreement
   - Partnership Agreement
   - Property Deal Agreement
3. **Customize** the template with your specific details

### Method 2: Custom Agreement
1. Choose "Custom Agreement" option
2. Start with a blank template
3. Add your own sections and terms

## ✏️ Agreement Creation Process

### Step 1: Basic Information
Fill in the essential details:
- **Agreement Title**: Descriptive name for your agreement
- **First Party Name**: Name of the first party (e.g., Employer)
- **Second Party Name**: Name of the second party (e.g., Employee)
- **Agreement Date**: Effective date of the agreement

### Step 2: Agreement Content
The main content area includes:

**Pre-filled Sections** (from templates):
- Each template comes with relevant legal sections
- Edit existing content to match your needs
- All text fields are customizable

**Dynamic Fields**:
- **Section Headers**: Editable titles for each section
- **Content Areas**: Detailed text for each agreement section
- **Add New Sections**: Create additional custom sections as needed

**Common Sections Include**:
- Position and Responsibilities (Employment)
- Compensation and Benefits
- Confidentiality Clauses
- Term and Termination
- Dispute Resolution

### Step 3: Review and Customize
- **Preview**: Review all sections before proceeding
- **Edit**: Make changes to any section
- **Validate**: Ensure all required fields are completed

## ✍️ Digital Signature Process

### Signature Requirements
Before finalizing any agreement, digital signatures are required:

**Signature Area**:
- Located at the bottom of the agreement
- Labeled for "Second Party" signature
- Touch-responsive signature pad

**How to Sign**:
1. Use your finger or stylus to draw your signature
2. Write naturally in the signature area
3. The signature is captured in real-time
4. Preview your signature before confirming

**Signature Features**:
- **Clear Button**: Erase and start over
- **Pressure Sensitivity**: Responds to drawing pressure
- **High Quality**: Captured at high resolution
- **Secure Storage**: Encrypted and stored securely

## 💾 Saving and Managing Drafts

### Save as Draft
If you're not ready to finalize an agreement:
1. Tap "Save as Draft" button
2. Your progress is saved locally and to the cloud
3. Continue editing later from the Dashboard

### Draft Management
- **Local Storage**: Drafts saved on your device
- **Cloud Backup**: Synchronized with your account
- **Auto-save**: Periodic automatic saving
- **Resume Editing**: Pick up where you left off

## ✅ Finalizing Agreements

### Complete Agreement Process
1. **Fill all required fields**
2. **Add digital signature**
3. **Review final agreement**
4. **Tap "Create" button**
5. **Confirm submission**

### After Submission
- Agreement status changes to "Complete"
- Unique agreement ID generated
- Available in your dashboard
- Can be viewed, downloaded, or shared

## 🚨 Troubleshooting

### Common Issues and Solutions

**Login Problems**:
- Verify email and password
- Check internet connection
- Reset password if needed

**Agreement Not Saving**:
- Ensure all required fields are filled
- Check internet connectivity
- Try saving as draft first

**Signature Issues**:
- Clear signature and try again
- Ensure you're drawing within the signature area
- Check device touch sensitivity

**App Performance**:
- Close and restart the app
- Check available device storage
- Update to latest app version

### Getting Help
If you encounter issues:
1. **Check this User Guide** for solutions
2. **Restart the Application** to resolve temporary issues
3. **Check Internet Connection** for sync problems
4. **Update the App** to the latest version
